# floors-win-over-the-issue-layer

## Decided by

jed72

## Date

2026-10-05

## Supersedes

Nothing.

## Decision

A floor wins over an issue's own configuration. An issue's `config:` cannot remove what a floor adds, as `approaches/rubric.md` already says for a person overriding a floor.

## Why

A floor exists for risk a single issue cannot judge away; letting the issue layer lift it would undo the floor.

## Evidence

The maintainer's answer to question Q-6 of the configurable-framework technical specification, 5 October 2026.
