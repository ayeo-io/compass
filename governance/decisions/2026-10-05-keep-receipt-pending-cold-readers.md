## Decision

The word "receipt" and the command `compass issue receipt` stay as they are until two or three first-time readers have been asked, before they see any output, what they expect the word to mean. Rename only if most of them misread it. Answers issue #116.

## Why

Chosen from the recommendation of 5 October. One reader's reaction is one data point, and a rename touches a public command, a frozen term and the demo script.
