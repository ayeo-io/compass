# ready-and-done-lists-are-configurable

## Decided by

jed72

## Date

2026-10-05

## Supersedes

Nothing.

## Decision

The Definition of Ready and the Definition of Done become configurable lists, per project and per issue. The shipped default carries today's seven Ready items and seven Done items word for word.

## Why

Carrying today's items unchanged keeps a project with no configuration behaving exactly as before.

## Evidence

The maintainer's answer to question Q-7 of the configurable-framework technical specification, 5 October 2026.
