## Decision

The verbs that write an issue's manifest print one line naming `compass issue dashboard` when the review page goes stale. They do not regenerate the page. Answers issue #121.

## Why

Chosen from the recommendation of 5 October. Regenerating would leave the dashboard-current check able to catch only a hand-edited page; a reminder keeps its reach and tells the person at the moment of acting.
