# evidence-types-stay-framework-owned

## Decided by

jed72

## Date

2026-10-06

## Supersedes

Nothing.

## Decision

Evidence types stay owned by the framework. A project cannot add, change or remove one in the first three releases of the configurable framework.

## Why

Typed evidence is one of the guarantees no configuration removes; letting a project define a type would let it define what counts as proof.

## Evidence

The maintainer's approval on 6 October 2026 of the recommendations for the open questions of the configurable-framework technical specification, given with the approval to split the configuration work into epics. Question Q-13.
