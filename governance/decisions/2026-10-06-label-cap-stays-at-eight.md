## Decided by

jed72

## Date

2026-10-06

## Supersedes

Nothing.

## Decision

The configuration classifier compares at most eight named labels. Above the cap it classifies a layer as incomparable and says why. The cap is revisited only with benchmark numbers.

## Why

Eight labels already give 307,200 grid points; a larger cap needs measured cost first.

## Evidence

The maintainer's approval on 6 October 2026 of the recommendations for the open questions of the configurable-framework technical specification, given with the approval to split the configuration work into epics. Question Q-19.
