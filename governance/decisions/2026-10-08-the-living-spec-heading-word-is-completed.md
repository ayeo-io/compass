# the-living-spec-heading-word-is-completed

## Decision

The living spec heads each issue `### <slug> (completed <date>)`. Only an issue that is `done` with close reason `completed`, on a delivery approach that ships, feeds the spec. The reader still accepts the older `landed` heading.

## Why

The section lists only completed issues. A `done` heading would suggest that `not-planned` and `duplicate` issues appear too. `completed` is also the close reason, so one word links the rule and the heading.
